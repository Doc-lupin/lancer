# The Basin — Official Map

Nov 4, 2026 · @Luna

## Crossroads layout

The Basin is four arms of very different size and character meeting at a single crossroads. Ostrom and Tallis sit one day apart at the meeting point. Ostrom opens spinward toward Gloam and rimward toward Scald, and Tallis opens coreward toward Pennant and trailing toward Halyard. Most traffic from one arm to another has to pass through one or both of them, which makes the pair the strongest point of control in the Basin.

Each box on the chart is a region of several stars joined by local lanes a few hours long, and only primary lanes are drawn. Every lane on the chart is labeled in lane days, from the one-day hop between Ostrom and Tallis to the eighteen-day run out of the Deeps.

The coreward arm runs up from Tallis, the trailing arm right from Tallis, the spinward arm left from Ostrom and the rimward arm down from Ostrom. Opposite arms have no bypass, so traffic between them goes through the crossroads.

### The arms

**Coreward.** The richest arm and the most settled. Pennant sits five days up the lane from Tallis, with Orison two days spinward of it and Wending three days trailing. Beyond them lie the Lantern Reach, nine days out from Pennant, the Pale, eight days from Wending, and the Orrery at the end of an eleven-day drifting lane from Orison. The Synod dominates the arm, but Corvel's claims run all through the Reach.

**Trailing.** A tight commercial knot. Halyard, Lumen and Sable are only a day or two from each other and three days from Tallis, close enough that the Free Lines, Smith-Shimano and the Sable markets work almost as one economy. The Furnace is a week out on a drifting lane from Lumen. The Hollows are ten days from Sable on a lane that runs only one way.

**Spinward.** Long and thin. Gloam is six days from Ostrom, the longest lane out of the crossroads, and the Purview holds it alone. Halvard lies two days beyond. The Shoals are four days from Gloam going in, but the only way out of them runs nine days to Halvard, and a tidal lane links them to the Orrery.

**Rimward.** The industrial arm, and the one at war. Scald is four days from Ostrom, with Ferrous two days spinward of it and Tarn three days trailing. Cairn sits five days further out on the road to the Deeps. From Cairn that road runs one way, and the only route home from the Deeps is eighteen days to the Boneyard.

### The bypasses

A handful of routes connect neighboring arms without passing through Ostrom or Tallis. None of them is easy, and each is held by someone the Stewardship would rather not depend on.

Opposite arms have no bypass at all. Anything moving between the spinward and trailing arms, or between coreward and rimward, goes through the crossroads.

### What the shape means

**The crossroads is a tollgate.** The Stewardship holds both Ostrom and Tallis, so it can inspect, tax or stop almost anything moving between arms. Its strength lies less in its fleet than in its position, and every faction in the Basin plans around it.

**Every bypass is a fight waiting to happen.** The Navy wants pickets on all of them, and the powers at their ends want them kept open. The war on Scald is a good example. The Purview's expeditionary force reaches the glassfields through the Halvard Gap without ever passing Ostrom, so Mercer-Ibarra decides how well supplied it is, and charges accordingly.

**The arms are not equal.** The coreward arm has the best land, the trailing arm the money, the rimward arm the lattice and the war, and the spinward arm the longest, loneliest lanes. Each is strong in something the others need, and each has to deal with the crossroads to get it.

**Ostrom is also where things arrive.** The Silent Mouths open into Ostrom from the Quiet. They lead nowhere anyone can follow, so their lane time is marked as unknown. If anything ever comes through, it arrives at the most heavily defended point in the Basin.
